## What this is

A free, open-source, text-based role-playing game that runs inside Discord.
There is nothing to buy. It is a hobby project, not a commercial service.

## Using it

You must meet Discord's own minimum age (13, or higher where your local law
says so) and follow the [Discord Terms of
Service](https://discord.com/terms) and [Community
Guidelines](https://discord.com/guidelines). Those apply to you whatever this
document says.

Play fairly and in good faith. Specifically, don't:

- **Exploit bugs.** If you find one that gives an unintended advantage, report
  it rather than farm it. Reporting a bug is welcome and has never cost anyone
  their character.
- **Automate play.** No self-bots, macros, or scripts driving commands.
- **Run multiple characters to advantage yourself.** One person, one
  cultivator. Alt accounts that trade with, vote for, or fight each other are
  not on.
- **Claim rewards you did not earn.** The vote reward assumes you actually
  voted; where the operator has configured it, that is checked.
- **Write things into the world that would not be acceptable to say in the
  channel.** Character names, chat and typed actions are all player-authored
  text that other people read.
- **Attack the service.** No attempts to overload it, gain unauthorised access,
  or reach data that is not yours.

## AI narration

Outcomes in this game are decided by deterministic code. The AI writes the
prose describing what already happened — it never decides rewards, deaths,
relationships or progression, and it cannot change your character.

AI-written text can still be wrong, odd, or repetitive. Treat it as flavour,
not as a statement of fact about the game state; where the narration and your
character sheet disagree, the sheet is right.

Narration is sent to a third-party model provider. See
[PRIVACY.md](PRIVACY.md) for exactly what is sent.

## Your characters and your data

Your character and its progress live in the operator's database. You can ask
for all of it to be deleted at any time — see
[PRIVACY.md](PRIVACY.md#deleting-your-data). Deletion is permanent and cannot
be undone.

Nothing in the game has real-world value. In-game currency, items and ranks
cannot be bought, sold, exchanged for anything real, or transferred off the
server, and you do not own them in any legal sense.

## Text you write

You keep whatever rights you have in the character names, actions and messages
you write. By writing them into the game you allow the operator to store them,
show them to other players, and pass them to the AI provider as narration
context, for the purpose of running the game.

## GM authority

The server's Game Masters can adjust, revive, move, freeze or reset characters,
and can remove anyone from the game. Every such action is written to an audit
log. GMs are people running a hobby server, not a support department.

## Availability

The bot is provided **as is**, with no guarantee of uptime, correctness, or
that your data survives. It runs on someone's home hardware. It can go down,
lose progress, roll back, or stop forever without notice. The world can be
reset. Keep nothing here you would be upset to lose — it is a game.

Features that depend on third parties (AI narration, Top.gg vote rewards) stop
working when those third parties do. The game is designed to keep running
without them, with plainer text.
